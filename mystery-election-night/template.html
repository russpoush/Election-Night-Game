Mystery Election Night

You're running the decision desk on a real presidential election night, any year from 1824 to 2024. Every state's final result is real. The catch: you don't know the year, and the candidates are disguised as colors.

  1. Tap a state to watch its count and call it. Correct and early pays more. A wrong call costs 50.
  2. Call the presidency when you're sure: up to 650 points, or minus 600 if you're wrong. It takes an Electoral College majority, and that number changes by era.
  3. Beat the rival network to a call for a scoop bonus.
  4. At the end, guess the year for 250 more. Then see who won and who they beat.

Clues: which states exist, how many electoral votes each has, how many candidates are running, and how the regions lean. Older years can have three, four or five candidates, and some states let the legislature pick the electors. The vote-counting order is simulated; the final results are real.

Tonight's mystery election is the same for everyone today, so you can each play and compare.
